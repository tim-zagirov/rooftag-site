ROOFTAG Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

ROOFTAG is a rooftop parkour game for iPhone and Android made by Tim Zagirov ("we"). This page explains what the game collects, why, and how to turn it off.

The short version

  • No accounts and no ads. We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it and we do not share it with data brokers.
  • Play stats are sent only if you say YES when the game asks. You can turn them off any time in Settings > SHARE STATS.
  • If the game crashes, a crash report is sent so we can fix the bug, whatever you chose for play stats.

Play stats (only if you say YES)

When you agree, the game sends these events through Unity Analytics:

  • a run started, finished or was left (restart, quit, or the app going to the background), with the track, the difficulty, the run time and the number of the attempt; for a left run, also the last checkpoint you reached;
  • how many swipes were Perfect, Good or rough and how early or late they were on average, how many falls, air tricks and spray cans, the style score, the TAGS you earned, whether it was a new best, and the race result against the Ink King (won or lost, by how many seconds, and whether you took his crown);
  • where on the track you fell (track section, obstacle type and checkpoint).

Unity Analytics adds technical data to these events:

  • an installation ID generated by Unity, and on iPhone your device's vendor identifier (IDFV: the same value for all apps from this developer on your device). Neither is your name, your Apple ID or your Google account, and neither is the advertising identifier. They tie the events to your installation and device, so the stats are not anonymous;
  • your approximate location, down to the country, worked out from your IP address;
  • device and performance data, for example: device model, iOS or Android version, game version, language, hardware details (CPU, GPU, memory, screen size and resolution), battery level and volume level.

If you say NO, or turn SHARE STATS off, none of this is sent.

Crash reports (always)

If the game crashes or hits an error, Unity Diagnostics sends a report with the technical cause (the crash stack and recent log lines) and device and operating system information (device model, iOS or Android version, game version). It contains no name or email. Crash reports are needed to keep the game working, so they are sent whatever you chose for play stats.

What we do not collect

Your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, the advertising identifier (IDFA) or anything you type. The game does not use the advertising identifier, so it does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

How the data is used

Only to find bugs and to make the game better: which tracks are too hard, where players fall, which difficulty feels right. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it and we do not share it with data brokers.

Who processes it

Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services) receives and stores the play stats and crash reports for us. See the Unity Privacy Policy. Unity keeps this data under its own retention rules.

Your choices

  • Turn play stats off: Settings > SHARE STATS > off. Nothing more is sent from that moment.
  • Ask us to delete your data: write to the address below. You cannot see your installation ID, so tell us roughly when you played and your phone model, and we will ask Unity to delete the data we can identify with that. You can also use the data deletion request path described in the Unity Privacy Policy.
  • Deleting the game removes everything it stored on your phone (settings and best times).

Children

ROOFTAG is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
